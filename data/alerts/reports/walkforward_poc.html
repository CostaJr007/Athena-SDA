For judges — plain language

What problem are we solving?

Earth orbit is crowded. Operators cannot stare at every satellite every day. Some objects quietly change how they fly — relocating in GEO, “shadowing” another satellite, or performing inspection-like approaches — long before a newspaper or think-tank writes about it.

Athena turns public orbital catalogs (TLE) and public space weather into a noise profile: a mathematical fingerprint of “is this object still behaving like itself?” When that fingerprint becomes rare compared with the object’s own history, we raise an alert for human analysts (and explain it with Bob).

What we claim

  • Real public TLE + GFZ weather (not fake orbits).
  • ML trained without future data at each test date.
  • Hard anomaly before open-source report anchors on 5/5 cases.
  • Same method on TERRA / NOAA-20 → no hard hits (controls).

What we do not claim

  • We do not “prove classified intent” or name spies.
  • XGBoost accuracy ≠ ground-truth HOSTILE label.
  • Lead-time is often a sustained regime, not one single burn.
  • Anchors are open publications / documented episodes, not secret TCA times.
One-sentence pitch for the jury: We download multi-year public orbits, describe noise with information-theory and change-detection math, train Isolation Forest only on the past, and show that Luch/Shiyan series looked statistically abnormal months before Gunter, CSIS, AMOS/SWF and Breaking Defense wrote about them — while civil satellites on the same days did not.

How the model “sees” trouble

Think of each satellite as a patient with a daily vital-signs chart (altitude, motion, irregularity). Athena builds a short medical summary of the last weeks, then asks: “Given only this patient’s past charts, is today’s chart weird?”

Mathematical foundation (why these numbers mean “noise”)

We do not only watch raw position. Public TLEs are already filtered products. The tactical signal is a change of regime in the time series — maneuvers, irregular station-keeping, shadowing, inspection-like control. Each tool answers a different question; Isolation Forest fuses them.

Theory Feature Plain meaning GEO inspection cue
Shannon entropy (1948) shannon_entropy_sma_30d How messy are altitude steps? Irregular burns spread the histogram
Hurst R/S (1951) hurst_exponent_sma Does the series keep drifting one way? H ≫ 0.5 → persistent low-thrust / control
Kolmogorov proxy kolmogorov_proxy_7d Is the up/down pattern hard to compress? Active control resists simple compression
L1-CUSUM l1_cusum_sma When did the series break? Localized break (often secondary in GEO folds)
ΔSMA / maneuvers delta_sma_7d_km, maneuver_count_30d How far / how often did altitude jump? Slot moves of tens of km, busy SK
Space weather (GFZ) f10_7, ap_index Is the Sun “stormy”? Separates LEO drag from intentional change
Isolation Forest anomaly_score Is the full profile rare vs past? Operational trigger (multivariate)
score(asof) = clip( 0.5 − IF.decision_function( x_asof ) )
train set = { windows ending before asof − holdout } // no future leak
hard hit ⇔ score ≥ 0.50 inside [t_peak − 45d, t_peak + 45d]
lead-time = t_peak − date(first score ≥ 0.50)

Controls · Placebos (same calendars)

Why placebos matter for the jury

If solar weather or “the whole sky was weird” caused our hits, civil satellites on the same dates would also hard-hit. They do not. That is the control arm of the experiment.

Scientific control: placebos share F10.7/Ap calendars with interest cases. Discriminator = orbital regime features (Hurst / Shannon / ΔSMA / IF joint profile), not the Sun alone.

Limits · scientific honesty

  • Long lead-times often mean a sustained anomalous regime from early in the window, not a single classified “prediction of intent.”
  • t_peak is an open-source publication / documented episode anchor — not a secret conjunction timestamp.
  • CUSUM is frequently low at first hit; Isolation Forest fires on the joint vector.
  • Pair risk supports prioritization; it is not full SGP4 TCA analysis.
  • Walk-forward hit criterion is the IF anomaly score, not XGBoost class accuracy.
