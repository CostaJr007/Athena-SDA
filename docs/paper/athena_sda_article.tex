\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We present \textbf{Athena-SDA}, a military-first Space Domain Awareness (SDA) system for \textbf{quantitative orbital noise analysis}, \textbf{micro-anomaly detection}, and \textbf{operational alerts} on platforms of interest (\textit{suspects}), using public TLEs and GFZ space weather.
We build a \textbf{quantitative feature vector} (Shannon entropy, multi-scale Hurst exponent, CUSUM, Kolmogorov proxy, stationarity tests, F10.7/Ap/Kp space weather, among others) and train an \textbf{Isolation Forest} on normality anchors (civil \textit{baseline} and protected \textit{assets}) under a \textbf{past-only} protocol.
We validate with \textbf{walk-forward} windows aligned to \textbf{open-source reports} and civil EO \textbf{placebo} controls.
On the GEO panel (Luch/Olymp-K, Shiyan-12, Luch-2): \textbf{5/5} hard hits at threshold $0.50$ with mean max score $\approx 0.65$, versus \textbf{0/7} hard hits and mean $\approx 0.46$ for civil EO placebos (gap $\approx 0.19$; one-sided Mann--Whitney $p\approx 0.001$).
A priority layer (XGBoost weak labels, suspect$\times$asset pairs, fuzzy rules) and the Bob copilot explain and rank attention without changing the quantitative score.
Short definitions of each mathematical tool are collected in the \textbf{Glossary} (Appendix~\ref{app:gloss}).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

SDA operators cannot monitor the full catalog with equal attention.
Athena-SDA focuses on \textbf{quantitative analysis of orbital time series} and \textbf{detection of noise / micro-anomalies} on military-interest objects, producing \textbf{alerts} and attention priority relative to platforms to protect.

The system combines:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item public data (multi-year TLEs + space weather);
  \item a deterministic quantitative noise framework (Sec.~\ref{sec:math});
  \item past-only Isolation Forest under military-first doctrine (Sec.~\ref{sec:if});
  \item walk-forward validation against report anchors and placebos (Sec.~\ref{sec:wf}--\ref{sec:results});
  \item priority and explanation layers (Sec.~\ref{sec:priority}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Research question.}
Does a quant + past-only Isolation Forest pipeline, trained on \textit{baseline}+\textit{asset} normality, separate military-interest windows aligned with open-source reports from civil EO controls under the same protocol?

\paragraph{Formal claims.}
\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=12pt]
  \item[Claim A.] Interest cases (open-source anchors) show elevated anomaly scores and/or hard hits near \(t_{\mathrm{peak}}\).
  \item[Claim B.] Civil EO placebos under the same protocol show lower scores and near-zero hard-hit rate at \(\mathrm{thr}=0.50\).
\end{description}

\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Orbital series (TLE)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Filtered history for 24 NORADs ($\sim$2014-01-01 to 2026-07-27), $\sim$\textbf{249{,}580} epochs.
  \item Sources: public TLE history mirror + daily CelesTrak GP ingest.
  \item Elements: semi-major axis (SMA), eccentricity, inclination, RAAN, mean motion, timestamps.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Space weather}
\textbf{GFZ Potsdam} (Kp, ap/Ap, observed/adjusted F10.7, sunspots):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \(F_{10.7}\), \(F_{10.7}^{\mathrm{adj}}\), \(\mathrm{Ap}\), \(\overline{K_p}\), sunspot number;
  \item 7-day means and deltas;
  \item \texttt{geomagnetic\_storm}$=1$ if \(\mathrm{Ap}_{\max,7\mathrm{d}}\ge 30\).
\end{itemize}
Space weather contextualizes atmospheric drag (especially LEO) relative to SMA changes.

\subsection{Open-source anchors}
Events in \texttt{events\_walkforward.json} with \((t_{\mathrm{start}}, t_{\mathrm{peak}}, t_{\mathrm{end}})\) and public sources (Gunter, CSIS, SWF, press, dual-use catalog notes). Examples: Luch/Olymp-K near Intelsat/Athena-Fidus; Shiyan-12; Luch-2; CSS Tianhe; placebos TERRA, AQUA, Landsat, NOAA.

\section{Quantitative noise vector}
\label{sec:math}

Let \(a(t)\) be the SMA series (km) on a window of \(n\ge 20\) epochs.
\texttt{src/engine.py} produces \(x\in\mathbb{R}^{d}\) for the Isolation Forest.
(See Appendix~\ref{app:gloss} for short tool definitions.)

\subsection{Shannon entropy}
Differences \(\Delta a_i = a_{i+1}-a_i\), histogram \(p_k\):
\begin{equation}
  H = -\sum_{k:\,p_k>0} p_k \log_2 p_k .
\end{equation}

\subsection{Kolmogorov complexity proxy}
Tokens \(\{U,D,S\}\) on \(\Delta a\) and zlib compression:
\begin{equation}
  K_{\mathrm{proxy}} = \mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\ell_{\mathrm{comp}}}{\ell_{\mathrm{raw}}},\,0,\,1\right).
\end{equation}

\subsection{Multi-scale Hurst (R/S)}
\(H\) on the full window and a short recent tip; gap
\begin{equation}
  \Delta H = H_{\mathrm{full}} - H_{\mathrm{short}}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Kernel \(L_1\)-CUSUM}
Accumulated deviations from local median / MAD, mapped to \([0,1]\).

\subsection{Mandelbrot / Hill tail}
Extreme-value score above a high quantile of the series.

\subsection{ADF}
Augmented Dickey--Fuller \(p\)-value on \(a(t)\).

\subsection{Deltas and maneuvers}
\(\Delta\)SMA over 7d/30d and CUSUM-peak maneuver counts.

\subsection{Topology and geometry (proxies)}
H0/H1 (proxy pairwise-distance mode), Chern--Simons proxy on \(h=r\times v\), Ollivier--Ricci proxy via local Wasserstein.
Reconstructed from orbital elements (not full SGP4 ephemerides).

\subsection{Space weather}
\(F_{10.7}\), Ap, Kp and 7-day derivatives in the same vector \(x\).

\subsection{Excluded from IF}
Distance to assets and cointegration feed the priority layer only.

\section{Past-only Isolation Forest}
\label{sec:if}

\begin{equation}
  s(x) = \mathrm{clip}\bigl(0.5 - \mathrm{decision\_function}(x),\, 0,\, 1\bigr).
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Training.}
Windows with \(\mathrm{window\_end}<\mathrm{cutoff}\); NORADs \textbf{baseline + asset}; contamination \(\sim 0.06\)--\(0.08\); \texttt{random\_state=42}; separate monitor and pipeline artifacts (\texttt{registry.json}).

\paragraph{Threshold.}
\begin{equation}
  thr = \max\bigl(0.50,\, p_{95}(s_{\mathrm{normality}})\bigr).
\end{equation}
Primary tables use \(\mathrm{thr}=0.50\).

\paragraph{Hard hit.}
Any fold with \(s\ge 0.50\) inside \([t_{\mathrm{peak}}\pm 45\,\mathrm{d}]\).

\section{Walk-forward protocol}
\label{sec:wf}

For each event \((t_{\mathrm{start}}, t_{\mathrm{peak}}, t_{\mathrm{end}})\):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \texttt{asof} grid with 14-day step.
  \item At each \texttt{asof}: fit IF only on normality windows ending before \texttt{asof}$-3$ days.
  \item Score the target NORAD on the current window.
  \item Metrics: hard hit, \(\max s\), pre-peak mean, \texttt{noise\_ramp}, \texttt{first\_fold\_hit}.
\end{enumerate}
Locked plan: \texttt{PROTOCOL\_PREREGISTRATION.md}.

\section{Priority layer}
\label{sec:priority}

Complements noise detection for operator attention ranking:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item XGBoost with weak labels (operational tiers);
  \item suspect$\times$asset distance and cointegration (\texttt{pair\_risk});
  \item Mamdani fuzzy rules and Kelly-style attention sizing;
  \item Bob: natural-language briefing from computed scores.
\end{itemize}
Claims A+B use only the walk-forward IF score.

\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:design}

\paragraph{GEO panel (primary).}
Interest: 5 events (Luch-1 $\times$3, SY-12, Luch-2), 3 unique NORADs.
Civil EO placebos: 7 windows (TERRA, AQUA, Landsat, NOAA), 5 unique NORADs.

\paragraph{Expanded panel.}
Up to 9 unique interest NORADs (LEO/MEO included); reported separately.

\paragraph{Separation test.}
One-sided Mann--Whitney \(U\) (interest $>$ placebo), emphasizing means and gap.

\subsection{Expanded panel}
With 11 events / 9 unique interest NORADs, hard-hit rate \(\approx 55\%\) (LEO not always \(\ge 0.50\)), with a positive mean gap vs placebos (MW \(p\approx 0.01\)). The primary result of this paper is the GEO panel.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Score \(s(x)\) responds to elevated noise regimes on GEO interest cases aligned with open-source anchors, with clear separation from civil EO controls.
Many cases show high pre-peak level and near-zero \texttt{noise\_ramp}, consistent with a \textbf{persistently elevated regime} in the analysis window.
Space weather contextualizes drag; pair risk and XGB rank attention when proximity to assets is present.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Small unique interest NORAD count; multi-event Luch-1 is dependent.
  \item Sparse, noisy TLEs; topology proxies on reconstructed geometry.
  \item LEO recon/dual-use hard hits less frequent at thr \(0.50\).
  \item XGB accuracy measures weak-label agreement, not Claims A+B.
\end{itemize}

\section{Glossary of mathematical and model tools}
\label{app:gloss}

Short definitions of \textbf{what each tool does} in Athena-SDA.

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=8pt,font=\normalfont\bfseries]
  \item[Shannon entropy]
  Measures how irregular or spread out altitude steps (\(\Delta\)SMA) are. High values: less predictable step patterns (irregular control / active ops).

  \item[Kolmogorov proxy]
  Estimates whether the up/down/stable sequence is hard to compress. High values: more elaborate or irregular maneuver patterns.

  \item[Hurst exponent (R/S)]
  Measures temporal persistence: whether deviations tend to continue in the same direction. \(H>0.5\): persistent control / micro-trajectory signature.

  \item[Multi-scale Hurst / \(\Delta H\)]
  Compares full-window persistence with the recent tip. Separates long memory from short-tip fluctuation.

  \item[\(L_1\)-CUSUM]
  Detects regime breaks by accumulating deviations from a recent robust level (median/MAD). High when the series changes baseline.

  \item[Mandelbrot / Hill tail]
  Highlights rare extreme jumps in the altitude series (impulsive events).

  \item[ADF (Dickey--Fuller)]
  Tests whether the series is stationary or drifts. High \(p\) favors non-mean-reverting regimes.

  \item[\(\Delta\)SMA and maneuver count]
  Quantify magnitude and frequency of semi-major-axis changes (relocation, intense ops).

  \item[Homology H0/H1 (proxy)]
  Summarizes structure of the reconstructed position cloud (connectivity / cycles across scale).

  \item[Chern--Simons (proxy)]
  Measures variation of specific angular momentum in reconstructed \(r,v\) --- non-Keplerian deviation indicator.

  \item[Ollivier--Ricci (proxy)]
  Measures local neighborhood distortion in the point cloud (discrete curvature).

  \item[Space weather (F10.7, Ap, Kp, storm)]
  Solar and geomagnetic context. Supports interpretation of LEO \(\Delta\)SMA under elevated drag.

  \item[Isolation Forest]
  Unsupervised model that isolates rare feature profiles. High score = atypical combination relative to normality anchors.

  \item[Past-only walk-forward]
  At each historical date the IF sees only the past. Scores interest and placebo event windows under that constraint.

  \item[Cointegration]
  Tests whether two altitude series share a common long-run trend (joint motion / escort pattern).

  \item[Suspect\(\times\)asset distance]
  Approximate orbital proximity for attention ranking near protected platforms.

  \item[XGBoost (weak labels)]
  Operational priority classifier from heuristic training targets. Does not define Claims A+B hard hits.

  \item[Mamdani fuzzy]
  Linguistic rules with soft membership for calibration under uncertainty (e.g.\ stale TLE).

  \item[Kelly]
  Attention/resource sizing heuristic from score and severity.

  \item[Bob]
  Text layer that describes computed scores and context; does not recompute the IF.
\end{description}
